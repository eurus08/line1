Notes:
  - 'warm' = data resident from a previous touch; repeated calls

  - 'cold' = cache deliberately flushed before every call (worst
    case, first-touch).
  - blas_axpy is 2 FLOPs and 24 bytes per element (read x,
    read y, write y) -- an even lower arithmetic intensity than
    dot, since axpy also pays a write cost dot never does.
  - Compare these GB/s figures directly against bench_dot's --
    they should land in a similar range, since both are bound
    by the same DRAM bandwidth ceiling, not by FLOPs.
